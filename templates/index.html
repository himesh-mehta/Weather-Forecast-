How does this work?

We ask 15 weather services (ECMWF, GFS, ICON, AccuWeather, WeatherAPI and others) for their forecast, then combine them.

  • The headline numbers are the median: the middle value once every forecast is lined up. A single odd forecast can't drag it off course.
  • Agreement is the share of forecasts close to that middle value (within 1.5 °C for temperature and 5 km/h for wind, and on the same side of rain vs. dry). High agreement means you can rely on the forecast more.
  • Some values are shown but not counted: forecasts that cover only part of the day, services that give only average wind instead of the maximum, and Windy's free trial data, which Windy deliberately scrambles.
  • Every service is always listed, in a fixed priority order. A "—" means that service doesn't provide that value. Nothing is estimated, interpolated or filled in.
